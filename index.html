Aurora Advance

Aurora Advance develops AI-assisted workflows for preparing, managing, and distributing digital content across supported platforms.

About this application

The Aurora Advance desktop workflow can prepare short-form video files and related metadata, and can connect to supported third-party platforms such as TikTok for authorized upload workflows.

When TikTok integration is enabled, users explicitly authorize access through TikTok. The application is designed to use only the permissions needed for the selected workflow.
