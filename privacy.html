Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

1. Information processed

When a user connects a supported third-party account, the Service may process information necessary to provide the requested integration. Depending on the platform and permissions granted, this may include account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization tokens, video files, captions, and upload-related metadata.

2. TikTok integration

For TikTok integration, Aurora Advance may request permissions such as basic account information and video upload access. These permissions are used only to identify the authorized account and to perform user-authorized video upload workflows.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare requested content, transmit content to supported platforms, record upload status, troubleshoot failures, and maintain the security and reliability of the Service.

4. Storage

The desktop workflow is designed to store credentials and operational data locally on the user’s device unless a third-party platform requires data to be transmitted to its own systems. Authentication secrets should be stored outside source-code repositories and should not be publicly disclosed.

5. Sharing

Aurora Advance does not sell personal data. Information may be transmitted to the third-party platform selected by the user, such as TikTok, as necessary to perform the requested action. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the Service.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored tokens, upload records, and related operational data may be retained for as long as needed to provide the Service or maintain reliable records. Users may revoke third-party authorization through the relevant platform and may delete locally stored credentials or related application data.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unauthorized access, including separating credentials from source code and limiting requested permissions to those needed for the intended workflow. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-party policies

Third-party services connected to Aurora Advance process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review those policies before authorizing access.

9. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Service, applicable law, or third-party platform requirements change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

Questions regarding privacy may be submitted through the official Aurora Advance contact channel designated by the operator.
